Why is climate education important?

Climate change affects people, communities and the environment. Education and awareness can help people understand these challenges and take action. Sustainable habits, responsible choices and solutions based on nature can all contribute to a healthier and more sustainable future. Learning about climate action helps us turn knowledge into practical decisions.
